        How it works

        Qloo scores how strongly people in each part of the city are drawn to your concept (affinity) and how popular places in the area are overall (popularity). Taste lift is the gap: affinity above what popularity predicts. It is a scouting signal, not a guarantee of demand. We rank the strongest, well-spread zones and count the matching places from a sample of the most popular ones within 1 km. Audience charts show Qloo affinity skew, not shares of customers.
