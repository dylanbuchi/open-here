      How it works

      Qloo scores how strongly people in each part of the city are drawn to your concept (affinity) and how busy the area is overall (popularity). Taste lift is the gap: high affinity with low general buzz means demand that nobody is serving yet. We rank the strongest, well-spread zones and count the competitors already within 1 km.
